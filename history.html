Timeline of Cell Discovery

  • 1595: Invention of the compound microscope by Zacharias Jansen.
  • 1665: Robert Hooke coins the term "cell" after observing cork under a microscope.
  • 1674: Anton van Leeuwenhoek discovers protozoa and bacteria as single-celled organisms.
  • 1838: Matthias Schleiden concludes that all plants are made of cells.
  • 1839: Theodor Schwann extends this to animals, forming the basis of cell theory.
  • 1855: Rudolf Virchow states that all cells come from pre-existing cells.

Cell Theory

Cell theory states: All organisms are composed of one or more cells; the cell is the basic unit of structure and organization; all cells come from pre-existing cells.
