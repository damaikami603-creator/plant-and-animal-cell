Structure of an Animal Cell

Animal cells are eukaryotic without a cell wall or chloroplasts. They have centrioles and smaller vacuoles. Key organelles include:

  • Cell Membrane: Controls entry/exit of substances.
  • Nucleus: Houses DNA.
  • Mitochondria: Energy production.
  • Lysosomes: Digestion of waste.
  • Golgi Apparatus: Processes proteins.
